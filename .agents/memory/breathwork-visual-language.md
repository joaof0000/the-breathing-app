---
name: Breathwork visual language
description: Confirmed direction for instructional and guided breathing visuals.
---

Use warm, abstract, organic motion for breathing guidance rather than anatomical body, belly, lung, or egg-like illustrations. The motion should feel like a recognizable expression of the Breathwork app, not a generic wellness orb.

**Why:** The anatomical belly-breathing image felt unappealing and out of place, while the user explicitly approved a candlelit expanding-light and ripple animation as the replacement.

**How to apply:** Favor ownable geometry, atmospheric material, or resonant linework with clear phase cues. Keep the effect subtle rather than intense, include a reduced-motion state, and preserve the refined amber-brown atmosphere.
