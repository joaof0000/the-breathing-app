---
name: Breathwork visual language
description: Confirmed direction for instructional and guided breathing visuals.
---

Use warm, abstract, organic motion for breathing guidance rather than anatomical body, belly, lung, or egg-like illustrations.

**Why:** The anatomical belly-breathing image felt unappealing and out of place, while the user explicitly approved a candlelit expanding-light and ripple animation as the replacement.

**How to apply:** For future breathing visuals, favor soft expansion, layered ripples, subtle light, and clear phase labels. Keep motion accessible with a reduced-motion state and preserve the app’s refined amber-brown atmosphere.
